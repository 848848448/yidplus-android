## New
- YID PLUS 2.0: the whole app is now built for phones, not a web page inside an app. It opens in well under a second and scrolls smoothly, even on small keypad phones
- Channels, Chats, Shorts, Music and Profile each have their own screen, and what you saw last shows straight away, even with no internet
- Shorts: the next video is ready before you get to it. Keypad: up/down for the next video, OK to pause, 1 like, 3 comments, 6 share
- Music keeps playing with the screen off, with play/pause/next on the lock screen and in notifications
- Notifications for new chat messages, likes and comments, new posts in channels you follow, and new Shorts from creators you follow
- Tablets: a side menu, and channels and chats open beside the list
- Profile → Check for updates

## Fixed
