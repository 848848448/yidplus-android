## New
- YID PLUS for Android: the whole site as a real app, full screen, with its own icon
- Stays signed in; Google sign-in works through the browser and comes back to the app
- Shorts, chats, channels and music full screen; videos go full screen and can turn sideways
- Pull down to refresh, an offline screen that reloads by itself when the internet is back
- Upload photos and videos from the gallery or the camera; downloads and music saving
- Share opens Android's share sheet; yidplus.com links open in the app
- Updates: the app checks for a new version on start and once a day, and installs it in one tap
